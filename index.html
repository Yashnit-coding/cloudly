GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Still deciding? Here are a few things customers often ask us.

Ask us anything ↗
How much does a website cost?

It depends on the pages, design, and features. We discuss your needs first and share a written quote before starting. Domain, hosting, and paid tools may cost extra.

How long will my website take?

A simple website can take a couple of weeks once the content and feedback are ready. Larger websites and custom web apps take longer; we will agree on a realistic timeline.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. We design responsive layouts and check how key pages look on phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Can I update the website later?

We can build an editing option where appropriate or help you make updates through an agreed support arrangement.

Do you make online stores and booking systems?

Yes, depending on the features needed. We will explain the options, costs, and any third-party services involved before work begins.

Do you provide maintenance?

We can agree on ongoing help for updates, backups, and fixes. The scope and response times should be confirmed in the project quote.
